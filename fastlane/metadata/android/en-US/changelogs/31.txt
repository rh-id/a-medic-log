Release 2.3.0+31

Features:
* Export a per-profile PDF medication report: medicines, schedules, adherence summary and intake history
* Search now also covers medicine and intake details and respects the selected profile
* Delete confirmations now show what will be removed along with the item

Fixes:
* Reminders show again on Android 13+ (notification permission was never requested)
* Reminders now use exact alarms: no more firing minutes late, and they survive reboots
* Doses only count as missed once their take window has closed
* App data is now excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer
